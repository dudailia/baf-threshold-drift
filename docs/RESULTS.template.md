All numbers below are generated by `src/trace.py` from the files in `results/`, produced by `make all` at predictions commit `{{value:predictions_commit|str}}`. Nothing is typed by hand; `make check` fails if a number does not trace to a results file or the run manifest.

How to read this file. The primary comparison is the budget miss, realized FPR minus the 5% target. TPR is always shown next to the realized FPR it was bought at, because every strategy applies a threshold to the same scores of the same month and so sits on that month's ROC curve. The oracle sets its threshold on the test month's own labels; it is an upper bound, not deployable. Seed spread (5 seeds) and bootstrap intervals (1,000 paired resamples of applications within a month, seed 0) are reported separately. A difference is called "differs" only if its paired 95% interval excludes 0 and the two marginal intervals overlap by less than half the shorter one; otherwise it is "inconclusive". Unless stated, numbers are LightGBM, 5% FPR target, older group `customer_age >= 50`, test months 4 to 7, with the model trained on months 0 to 2 and the static threshold set on month 3.

## 9. Limits

The shuffled-label check failed its declared tolerance; the diagnostics found no leak, the cause of the failure is not established, and the check as declared did not pass. Data origin: BAF is synthetic. The Kaggle page says that "to protect the identity of potential applicants we have applied differential privacy techniques (noise addition), feature encoding and trained a generative model (CTGAN)" (Kaggle dataset page, description, list item "Privacy preserving"); the datasheet says "The presented datasets are sampled from a generative model (CTGAN)" (Datasheet, answer A6) and "we added Laplacian noise to the original data, with varying privacy budgets (inverse of the noise scale)" (Datasheet, answer A14). Month-to-month shifts in these files may therefore reflect the generator and how each variant was sampled, not the behaviour of the bank's applicants. The six variants share one generator, so the results are about this suite and one split (train 0 to 2, validate 3, test 4 to 7), not about fraud systems in general. The model was tuned over 20 configurations and trained once on months 0 to 2; retraining was studied only post hoc (RESULTS_POSTHOC.md section 4). Thresholds are placed just above the highest unflagged score; placing them at the lowest flagged score changes {{value:check/independent/n_rows_differing|int}} of {{value:check/independent/n_rows|int}} static and delayed-label rows, at most in the third decimal. The validation-alert-rate strategy assumes the fraud rate stays close to the validation month's; it drifted over budget where the fraud rate fell below month 3's and under budget where it rose. The contribution is a measurement on BAF and a comparison of threshold strategies by budget miss, month by month; that a fixed threshold drifts is already known (reports/related_work.md).
